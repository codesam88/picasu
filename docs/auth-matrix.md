## Columns

Each row names one operation (Op) and its handler (Name): Role is who may call (admin, user, share holder, or — for token-only and public operations), Tokens is the credential decoded (session cookie, snapshot or asset bearer, share headers), Scope is the share binding (own album or —), and Guards lists the request guards verbatim.
